- keyboard shortcuts can now be viewed and edited in the settings.
- auto saving as you write can now be turned off, and a new save catch is implemented for if you try to leave an unsaved note.
- Reminders:
	reminder: 13:50 fires a reminder at that time.
	reminder: 2026-12-25 fires a reminder at that date.
	reminder 2026-12-25 07:00 fires a reminder at that time on that date.
reminders don't run in the background, so the app only remind you subtly when you have the app running.
